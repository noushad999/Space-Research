In one sentence

A spice classifier trained on studio photographs scores 100% on studio photographs and loses almost 38 points on everyday photographs of the same spices, while a model trained on everyday photographs moves to the studio almost for free.

Every architecture collapses the same way

Eight models, light and heavy, convolutional and attention-based. All reach about 100% within the studio source; all lose 32 to 86 points when moved to everyday photographs. The reverse cost stays small for most.

What a single training source can recover

Held-out accuracy (studio → in-the-wild) for ordinary training and five interventions. Bars show ±1 std over three seeds; hollow dots are single-seed ablations. The elaborate method and a one-line logit penalty land in the same place.

ARC-V vs ERM, pooled over three seeds: McNemar b = 359, c = 155, p = 3.4×10⁻¹⁹. ARC-V and spectral decoupling cannot be separated at three seeds.
